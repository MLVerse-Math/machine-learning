# 🎯 Events

## Overview

After defining the **Sample Space**, the next step in probability theory is understanding **Events**.

An **event** is a collection of one or more outcomes from a sample space. Events allow us to ask meaningful probability questions such as:

- What is the probability of rolling an even number?
- What is the probability of drawing a red card?
- What is the probability that tomorrow will be rainy?
- What is the probability that an email is spam?

Events are fundamental to probability theory and serve as the building blocks for conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, random variables, probability distributions, and machine learning models.
