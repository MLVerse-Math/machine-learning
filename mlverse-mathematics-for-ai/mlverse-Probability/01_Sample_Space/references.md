# 🎯 Recommended Learning Path

1. Understand **Sample Spaces** and **Events**.
2. Learn **Conditional Probability**.
3. Study **Bayes' Theorem**.
4. Explore **Random Variables**.
5. Master **Probability Distributions**.
6. Learn **Joint Probability** and **Independence**.
7. Understand **Markov Models**.
8. Apply probability concepts to **Machine Learning** and **Artificial Intelligence**.

---

# 📌 Key Takeaway

A strong understanding of **Sample Space** is the first step toward mastering probability theory. Every probabilistic model, statistical algorithm, and AI system begins by defining the universe of possible outcomes. The references above provide a comprehensive pathway from foundational probability to advanced applications in modern Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science.
