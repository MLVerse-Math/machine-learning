# Derivatives

## Overview

Derivatives are one of the most important concepts in Calculus and Machine Learning.

A derivative measures the instantaneous rate of change of a function with respect to its input.

Derivatives help answer questions such as:

- How fast is a quantity changing?
- What is the slope of a curve at a point?
- How do neural networks learn?
- How does Gradient Descent optimize a model?

Derivatives form the mathematical foundation of:

- Optimization
- Gradient Descent
- Deep Learning
- Backpropagation
- Reinforcement Learning
