# Hessian Matrix

## Overview

The **Hessian Matrix** is the matrix of second-order partial derivatives of a scalar-valued function. While the Jacobian describes the first-order rate of change, the Hessian reveals how that rate of change itself changes, providing information about **curvature**, **convexity**, and **optimization**.

The Hessian is one of the most important mathematical tools in:

- Machine Learning
- Deep Learning
- Numerical Optimization
- Scientific Computing
- Computer Vision
- Robotics
- Large Language Models

It enables algorithms to determine whether a point is a **minimum**, **maximum**, or **saddle point**, making it indispensable for optimization.

---

# Why Study the Hessian Matrix?

Optimization is the heart of Artificial Intelligence.

Training a neural network means minimizing a loss function:

$$
L(\theta)
$$

The Hessian helps answer:

- Is this point a minimum?
- How curved is the loss landscape?
- How quickly does the function change?
- Which optimization algorithm should be used?
