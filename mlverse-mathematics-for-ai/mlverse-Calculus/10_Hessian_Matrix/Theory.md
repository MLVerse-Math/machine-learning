# Hessian Matrix Theory

## What is the Hessian Matrix?

The Hessian Matrix is a square matrix containing all second-order partial derivatives of a scalar-valued function.

It measures how curvature changes in different directions and is fundamental to optimization.

---

## Mathematical Definition

For

$$
f(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n)
$$

the Hessian is

$$
H=
\left[
\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_i \partial x_j}
\right]
$$

---

## Why It Matters

Unlike gradients, which indicate direction, the Hessian measures the shape of the surface.

It helps identify:

- Local minima
- Local maxima
- Saddle points
- Curvature

---

## Intuition

Imagine walking on a mountain.

The gradient tells you which direction is uphill.

The Hessian tells you how steeply the terrain bends beneath your feet.
