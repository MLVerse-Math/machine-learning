# Chain Rule

## Overview

The Chain Rule is one of the most important concepts in Calculus and Artificial Intelligence.

Most real-world functions are composed of multiple nested functions:

$$
y = f(g(x))
$$

The Chain Rule helps compute derivatives of such composite functions.

Without the Chain Rule:

- Neural Networks cannot learn
- Backpropagation cannot work
- Deep Learning cannot exist
- Large Language Models cannot be trained

---

## Why It Matters?

Modern AI systems are built from layers of transformations:

Input → Hidden Layers → Output → Loss

Each layer is a function.

The Chain Rule allows gradients to flow backward through these layers.
