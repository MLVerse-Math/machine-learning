# Partial Derivatives in Artificial Intelligence

## Gradient Descent

Updates parameters using:

$$
\theta
=
\theta
-
\eta
\nabla L
$$

---

## Neural Networks

Backpropagation computes partial derivatives of loss functions with respect to weights and biases.

---

## Deep Learning

Training relies on gradients of millions of parameters.

---

## Computer Vision

CNN optimization uses partial derivatives.

---

## Reinforcement Learning

Policy gradients depend on partial derivatives.

---

## Large Language Models

Transformer training uses gradients computed through partial differentiation.
