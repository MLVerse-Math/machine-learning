# Taylor Series

## Overview

The **Taylor Series** is one of the most powerful tools in mathematics. It allows complex functions to be approximated using polynomials around a specific point. This idea forms the mathematical foundation of numerical analysis, optimization, differential equations, scientific computing, machine learning, and deep learning.

Taylor Series enables computers to efficiently approximate nonlinear functions, making it indispensable in modern AI systems.

---

# Why Study Taylor Series?

Many mathematical functions such as:

- Exponential
- Logarithm
- Sine
- Cosine
- Sigmoid
- Softmax

cannot always be evaluated analytically in optimization.

Taylor Series provides a polynomial approximation that simplifies computation.
