# Taylor Series Theory

## What is Taylor Series?

Taylor Series approximates a smooth function using an infinite polynomial expansion around a chosen point.

Instead of evaluating a complicated function directly, we approximate it using derivatives.

---

## Mathematical Definition

For a function

$$
f(x)
$$

expanded around

$$
a
$$

the Taylor Series is

$$
f(x)=
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}
\frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x-a)^n
$$

---

## Why It Matters

Taylor Series is used in:

- Numerical computation
- Optimization
- Differential equations
- Scientific simulations
- Machine Learning
- Deep Learning

---

## Intuition

Imagine replacing a complicated curve with a polynomial that closely matches it near a point.

The more derivatives we include, the better the approximation.
