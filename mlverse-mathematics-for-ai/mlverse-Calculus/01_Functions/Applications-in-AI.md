# Functions in Artificial Intelligence

## Activation Functions

Neural networks use functions such as:

$$
ReLU(x)
=
\max(0,x)
$$

---

## Sigmoid Function

$$
\sigma(x)
=
\frac{1}{1+e^{-x}}
$$

Used in binary classification.

---

## Loss Functions

Measure model error.

Example:

$$
MSE
=
\frac{1}{n}
\sum(y-\hat{y})^2
$$

---

## Feature Transformations

Functions help create useful features.

Examples:

- Log Transform
- Exponential Transform
- Scaling Functions

---

## Neural Networks

A neural network is a composition of functions:

$$
f(g(h(x)))
$$
