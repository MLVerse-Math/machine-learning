# Continuity in Artificial Intelligence

## Neural Networks

Continuous activation functions enable gradient-based learning.

Examples:

- Sigmoid
- Tanh
- Softplus

---

## Optimization

Gradient Descent assumes smooth behavior of the loss function.

---

## Computer Vision

Continuous transformations are used in image processing.

---

## Reinforcement Learning

Continuous value functions improve policy learning.

---

## Deep Learning

Training stability depends heavily on continuity.
