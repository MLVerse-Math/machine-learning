# Final Note

Integrals are one of the most powerful concepts in mathematics. They form the mathematical foundation of accumulation, probability, expectation, optimization, and continuous systems. From calculating area under a curve to training advanced AI models, integration serves as a bridge between classical calculus and modern artificial intelligence.
