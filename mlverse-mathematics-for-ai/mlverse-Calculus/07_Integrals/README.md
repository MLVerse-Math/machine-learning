# Integrals

## Overview

If derivatives tell us how things change, integrals tell us how things accumulate.

Integration is one of the fundamental operations in Calculus and plays a critical role in:

- Machine Learning
- Deep Learning
- Probability Theory
- Statistics
- Physics
- Optimization
- Scientific Computing

Integrals help answer questions such as:

- What is the area under a curve?
- How much quantity has accumulated?
- What is the expected value of a probability distribution?
- How are continuous probability models evaluated?

---

## Why Integrals Matter in AI?

Modern AI relies heavily on probability and optimization.

Integrals are used in:

- Probability Density Functions (PDFs)
- Bayesian Learning
- Expected Value Computation
- Reinforcement Learning
- Continuous Optimization
- Diffusion Models
- Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)
