# Applications in Artificial Intelligence

Multivariable Calculus appears throughout Artificial Intelligence.

## Machine Learning

Loss functions depend on multiple parameters:

$$
L(w_1,w_2,\ldots,w_n)
$$

---

## Deep Learning

Neural networks compute gradients across millions of parameters.

---

## Computer Vision

Images are naturally multivariable functions.

---

## Reinforcement Learning

Value functions depend on many state variables.

---

## Robotics

Robot motion planning uses multivariable optimization.

---

## Large Language Models

Training LLMs requires optimization over billions of parameters using gradients derived from multivariable calculus.
